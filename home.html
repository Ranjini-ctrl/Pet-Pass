About PetPass

A Digital Identity for Your Furry Family

Just like humans carry IDs, your pet deserves one too. PetPass creates a secure, shareable digital identity that travels with your pet everywhere.

🐾

Every pet has a story worth keeping

PetPass is a modern digital identity platform built for pet owners who care deeply about their companions. We believe every pet deserves a verified, accessible record of their life — from their first vet visit to their latest vaccination.

🔒

Secure & Private

Your pet's data is encrypted and only accessible to you and trusted vets.

📱

Always Accessible

Access your pet's full profile from any device, anywhere in the world.

❤️

Built with Love

Designed by pet lovers, for pet lovers. Every feature is crafted with care.

Features

Everything Your Pet Needs

A complete digital toolkit for modern pet ownership — all in one beautiful card.

🪪

Pet Identification

A verified digital ID with your pet's photo, name, breed, and unique PetPass number for instant identification.

💉

Vaccination Tracking

Keep all vaccination records organized with due-date reminders so you never miss an important shot.

Medical Records

Digital Pet Identity

Pets Deserve IDs Too

Humans carry passports, driver's licenses, and health cards. Why should our pets be any different? A digital identity gives your pet a voice when they can't speak for themselves.

  • Instant identification at vet clinics
  • Proof of ownership in disputes
  • Emergency medical access via QR
  • Travel documentation support
  • Lost pet recovery assistance
  • Vaccination proof for boarding
